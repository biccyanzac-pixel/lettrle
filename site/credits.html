Credits and licences

Guesses are checked against the ENABLE word list, which is in the public domain, together with SCOWL (Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists) by Kevin Atkinson, wordlist.aspell.net.

The answers are common words picked from SCOWL's size levels, which rank words by how common they are: Monday's come from the most common level and Saturday's from rarer ones, then curated by hand.
